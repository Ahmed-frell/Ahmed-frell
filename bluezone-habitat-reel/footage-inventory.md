# Blue Zone Habitat: footage and asset inventory (verified 2026-11-06)

All files were downloaded from the client's Google Drive into `media/drive/` (not committed) and inspected frame by frame.
Contact sheets are in `work/sheets/`.

**Authenticity:** every architectural video and image is a **3D architectural visualisation / render**, not footage of built
construction. Every scene that uses them carries an on-screen "Artist's impression" tag. The only real camera footage is
the five presenter clips, which are not used in V2.

## 3. Typography system (observed)
1. **Primary:** rounded geometric sans, bold, white, slight drop shadow (Poppins/Quicksand family look).
2. **Emphasis:** heavy condensed/compressed display, brand red (Anton/Bebas look), sometimes stencil-cut or 3D-extruded.
3. **Accent:** connecting script, used for one word per phrase ("actually", "shown", "you're").
4. **Behaviours:** word-by-word pop with an overshoot scale (≈ 0.6 → 1.08 → 1.0, about 6–8 frames); words placed on **curved paths and 90° rotations**; **tracking expansion** on reveal; **glow** on key white words; hand-drawn **marker annotations** (circles, corner brackets); text interleaved with the subject (behind/in front).

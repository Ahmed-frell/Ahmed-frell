# Reference analysis — D'Homz "Laventa" villa reel (35.8 s)

Specs: 720×1280 (9:16), 24 fps, H.264, AAC 48 kHz stereo. No hard cuts: every change is a motion transition.

## Style DNA
- **Pace:** 15 scenes in 30 s, about 2 s each, with each change on a music hit. A 5 s logo outro follows.
- **Story:** question hook → location → land value → "No.1 project" claim → interiors and configuration → amenities → lifestyle → sketch-to-reality → CTA → logo.
- **Look:** warm, bright, high-key. Golden-hour aerials and clean blue skies. A cream and beige palette with deep-green (pin) and violet (neon and icons) accents.
- **Type:** Malayalam bold sans and a big serif "VILLA" for the hook. A geometric sans (Poppins or Montserrat style) for claims. Neon-glow violet labels on aerials. Thin-line violet icons on frosted-glass cards.
- **Transitions:** bloom or flash whites, a tilted whip-blur, split-screen panels sliding in, a pink light leak, and a sketch-to-render wipe.
- **Audio:** an upbeat music bed at a steady −12 LUFS with no gaps, probably no voiceover. Whooshes on transitions. The level drops at 30.5 s for the logo.

## Bluezone Habitat version (original, not a copy)
Same structure and energy, with Bluezone's own message, palette and shots:
- **Palette:** Bluezone blues and teals with a warm sand neutral, instead of beige, green and violet.
- **Hook:** a question in Malayalam on Bluezone's own angle (to confirm: location, price or lifestyle). Use a different type pairing from the reference and a location pin in Bluezone colours.
- **Shots:** location aerial with tracked labels for nearby landmarks → plot or site outline → hero villa with a "Habitat" claim card → interiors grid → amenity glass cards (Bluezone's real USPs) → lifestyle split screens → plan-to-real wipe if you have a floor plan or elevation drawing → CTA pill → Bluezone logo outro.
- **Transitions:** keep the beat-synced motion, but use water ripples and blue light leaks in place of the pink leak and cream flashes.
